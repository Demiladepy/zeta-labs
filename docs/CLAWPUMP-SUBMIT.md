## Short description (paste)

Zeta Labs is policy-bounded credit for Solana agents: lenders fund a program-owned
vault; agents spend only under on-chain cap, expiry, and revoke; settlement rides
Payment Channels. For the ClawPump track we wire a ClawPump agent
(`zeta-credit-agent`) whose persona is Zeta credit rails — the spend proof is our
live Devnet vault/policy programs + seven-step spend-submit, not a mock dashboard.

We are **not** claiming pump.fun launch, paid DEX trading, or marketplace bounties
in this packet.
